Nothing here is tuned on P&L: every variant uses the fixed 4.5-cent edge threshold, the 3-second
persistence rule and a fill one second after the signal.

## Variants

- **baseline**: The frozen rules (2026-11-12 11:00 UTC): staleness in the error bars, 2.5-bar limit, two or three contracts, held to expiry.
- **brief_bars**: The brief's error bars: tick and line error only, no allowance for our stale spot price.
- **limit_4**: Disagreement limit 4.0 error bars, the lowest limit that signals on at most 1% of seconds.
- **three_only**: Trade only when three contracts vote, so the mispriced one can be named.
- **shared_stale**: The 5m and 15m share one Chainlink price, so a stale price moves both votes at once. Their combined error bar uses that shared error instead of treating it as independent.
- **bias_corrected**: Each contract's vote is divided by its typical width relative to the 5m, measured on earlier events only (the 15m prices about 15% wider than the 1h).
- **bias_shared**: bias_corrected and shared_stale together.
- **exit_30s**: Baseline entries, but sold back at bid/ask 30 seconds after entry instead of held to expiry.
